# CareerLens

CareerLens is:

- **Unified job feed** - aggregates vacancies from multiple job sources in one place
- **Your AI assistant** - connects your personal assistant (Cursor, Claude Code, Antigravity, and others) to the service via MCP
- **Smart AI scoring and chat** - in a live conversation, your assistant answers ad-hoc queries, scores vacancies against your resume, preferences, and English level, and saves the results back to the service
- **Autopilot** - background job search matching your interests with notifications
- **Distraction-free viewing** - clean interface focused on fast, productive job hunting without clutter
- **Job search tracking** - saved vacancies, application tracking, and personal notes for each role
- **Precise filters** - filter by companies, salary range, publication dates, and other criteria
